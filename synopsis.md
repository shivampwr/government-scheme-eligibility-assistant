1. TITLE OF THE PROJECT
------------------------------------------------------------

Government Scheme Discovery & Eligibility Assistant

A Hybrid Multi-Agent AI Framework for Citizen Empowerment that flips the paradigm from citizens searching for schemes to the system finding schemes for the citizen using a dual-engine approach consisting of a local RAG knowledge base, Playwright DOM Scraper, Serper API Web Search, and an interactive React web dashboard.


------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Millions of citizens, especially those from marginalized or rural communities, fail to benefit from government welfare programs simply because they are unaware of their existence or confused by bureaucratic language defining eligibility.

Scheme details are updated frequently and scattered across fragmented departmental portals and dense PDF notifications. A citizen must manually cross-reference their personal demographics such as income, caste, gender, occupation, location, age, and other criteria against dozens of overlapping schemes just to determine whether they qualify.

This creates an information gap between government welfare programs and the citizens who need them.


------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHY IS THE PARTICULAR TOPIC CHOSEN?
------------------------------------------------------------

The core challenge in public welfare is the lack of accessible, understandable, and up-to-date information.

This topic was chosen because it sits at the intersection of "Tech for Good" and Artificial Intelligence. By combining a local RAG Knowledge Base of government schemes with Playwright-based web scraping and Serper API Web Search, the proposed system aims to provide citizens with relevant scheme information based on their personal profile.

The system reduces the need for citizens to manually search multiple government portals and simplifies complex eligibility requirements into easy-to-understand information.


------------------------------------------------------------
4. OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE OF THE PROJECT
------------------------------------------------------------

OBJECTIVES

• Natural Language Profile Extraction:
Allow citizens to explain their situation in their own natural language through text or voice.

• AI-Based Scheme Retrieval:
Retrieve relevant government schemes from the local knowledge base and, when required, search official government sources using web search.

• Dynamic Eligibility Analysis:
Use AI-based policy reasoning to compare the user's demographic profile with scheme eligibility criteria and identify potentially eligible and ineligible schemes.

• Explain and Guide:
Summarize schemes in simple language, provide application guidance, and generate required document checklists.

• Multilingual and Voice Support:
Support translation into regional Indian languages and provide voice-based interaction.

• Citizen Assistance:
Provide a conversational interface through which users can ask questions about government schemes and receive understandable responses.


SCOPE

The initial prototype focuses on conversational scheme discovery, user profile extraction, hybrid information retrieval, eligibility analysis, document requirements, application guidance, and an interactive React.js web dashboard.

The system consists of multiple functional components including Profile Extraction, RAG Retrieval, Web Scraping, Web Search, AI Eligibility Analysis, and Citizen Counseling.

Future scope includes integration with government verification services such as DigiLocker and deployment through messaging platforms such as WhatsApp.

6. CONTRIBUTION TOWARDS SOCIETY
------------------------------------------------------------

The proposed system aims to make government welfare information easier to discover and understand.

By simplifying complex eligibility conditions and providing scheme information in an accessible conversational format, the system can help citizens identify government programs that may be relevant to their circumstances.

Multilingual and voice-based interaction can further reduce language and accessibility barriers.

The project aims to bridge the information gap between government welfare schemes and citizens by providing a centralized AI-assisted platform for scheme discovery, eligibility understanding, documentation requirements, and application guidance.

8. CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------

The Government Scheme Discovery & Eligibility Assistant proposes a hybrid AI-based solution for improving access to government welfare information.

By combining Retrieval-Augmented Generation, government scheme data, web scraping, web search, and AI-based eligibility analysis, the system provides citizens with a single platform for discovering relevant government schemes.

The conversational interface simplifies complex government information and provides users with scheme summaries, eligibility information, document requirements, and application guidance.

The project demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can be applied to address real-world public information challenges and improve accessibility to government welfare services.
